## Connecting gaps

**G1 · high · "Ridge: wafer compute-bound on paper" + "decode limited by kernel read rate" + "GEMM limited by per-PE instruction efficiency".** These three findings sit in three different sections, and the page never joins them.
- The ridge widget says decode is above the wafer's ridge.
- The decode callout says reads at the "measured f16 matrix-vector kernel" rate set the pace.
- The GEMM paragraph says f32 GEMM reaches 32% because of bank clashes and ≈37 cycles per instruction.

The missing bridge is that on the wafer neither memory nor fabric is the limit for these kernels. What limits them is what the per-PE DSD loop sustains:
- decode: 7.5 B/cycle ≈ 4 MAC/cycle against the 8-wide f16 peak (note 27);
- GEMM: 32% of the f32 ideal (Ch 14);
- the op rates come from Ch 6 and note 16.

That is the wafer's real analogue of "bandwidth-bound", and it is the course's most original lesson. Fix: one short paragraph after the decode callout. Optionally add the "what limits this kernel" table that ch15-fixlog D4 left partial: decode / stencil / GEMM / CG × GPU / wafer. Effort M.

This page states the "60 streams" and "KV cache shares the 44 GB" points but never joins them. At 13 streams the widget gives ≈ $2.7/M. Fix: add a KV-limited stream cap with a context input, and rewrite the paragraph and the open question. Effort M.
