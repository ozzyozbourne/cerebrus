## Gaps, ranked

**G1 · high · Ch 4 #latency, last sentence of the method paragraph.** The page says "A CE that adds as well as relays (Lab 2) costs about 7 cycles per PE [Measured, Ch. 12]". Ch 12 (#hop-cost) now says that ~7.2 (WSE-3) / 5.0 (WSE-2) is an own-clock artifact. The true CE-forwarded hop is ≈ 9.2 / 6.0 cycles (CCN 24), and Ch 12 uses that value as c. A learner who takes Ch 4's number underestimates chain reductions by ~25%. *Resolve:* "about 9 cycles per PE on WSE-3 and 6 on WSE-2 (router hop + ramps + @fadds), measured as a round trip in Ch 12 #hop-cost". Writing only. Effort S.

**G2 · high · Ch 3 #pe-speed and #wse2-wse3 table (f16 rate and its explanation).** Ch 3 gives f16 multiply-add as ≈3.2/cycle (WSE-3) and ≈2.0/cycle (WSE-2). It explains the shortfall from 8/4 lanes as "likely … memory traffic". Later measurements contradict both the rate and the reason. Note 22 gives f16 fmach 4/cycle on WSE-3. Note 29 and App E give WSE-2 f16 @fmach over 1,024 elements at 270 cycles (≈3.8/cycle) when the operand streams start 4 banks apart, and 526 cycles otherwise. The memory traffic is the same in both cases, so placement, not traffic, sets the rate. Ch 6 already says "3.2–4 depending on layout". Ch 3 (and the App B warp row, which copies Ch 3's inference) is now stale. *Resolve:* change the table cell to "≈2–3.8 / ≈3.2–4, layout-dependent". Replace the inference with "operand bank placement (Ch 6, App E)". Optionally, re-time the f16 matvec on WSE-2 with A shifted by 4 banks and report the result. Resolvable: writing only, plus simulator for the matvec rerun. Effort S (text) / M (rerun).

**G6 · low-med · Ch 4 #color-budget ("reserves 6 colors … not broken down").** This can be resolved by arithmetic. The README's sentence is "21 colors and task IDs … SdkRuntime reserves 6 … only 4 left", and 21 + 6 + 4 = 31. So the README counts IDs in the 5-bit activatable space (0–30; sys_params WARNING 1/2: ".activate only accepts color 0~30"), not the 24 routable colors. The 6 are memcpy's 4 colors plus its entrypoints. *Resolve:* replace the parenthetical with this reconciliation and tag it Derived. Writing only (the SDK source is quoted). Effort S.
